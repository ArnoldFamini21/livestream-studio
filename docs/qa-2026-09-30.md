# QA audit — September 30, 2026

Baseline: `9c0fc0a` (main). Goal: find what still separates the studio from StreamYard and Riverside, fix what can be fixed safely now, and rank the rest.

## Method

| Step | What was exercised |
| --- | --- |
| Automated suites | shared, server, media server, client, deployment scripts; typecheck and production build of every workspace |
| Live browser run | Chromium (Playwright) with fake camera and microphone against local signaling, media, and Vite servers: studio creation, host entry, guest join, green room, admit, two-way video, chat both ways, guest leaving, every host panel, the More menu, Invite, keyboard shortcuts, 390 px phone guest and phone workspace |
| Recording | Record 12–14 s with a guest on stage, stop, library contents, MP4 export; end the room in the middle of a take |
| Go live | Custom RTMP destination pointed at a local FFmpeg RTMP listener; live ticks, watch page, End while live |
| Live relay under stalls | The real encoder, slate, and program splicer, driven with WebM input that stalls for 8 s (delayed delivery, and video frames missing for 8 s) |
| Icon scan | Every button icon in the studio, panels, tools, green room, and workspace checked for collapsed size |

## Fixed in this batch

| Area | Finding | Correction and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ending the session** | The host's **End** button ended the room for everyone with one click and no confirmation, even while live or recording. | A confirmation now names what ends (broadcast, recording, the other people in the studio) and defaults focus to **Keep studio open**. Browser check: cancel keeps the studio and the recording running. |
| **Recording lost at End** | Ending the room mid-take never finalized it. The take was missing from the library; only a 438 KB audio fragment appeared under **Recover recording**. | Confirming stops and saves the recording first, then waits up to two minutes for guests' uploads of that take (their final upload uses the studio connection), then closes the room. **End now** skips the wait. Browser check: the ended take is in the library with its program and individual tracks. |
| **End countdown** | During the 10-second countdown the host could not change their mind, and the host was told "The host is ending this session." | New host-only `cancel-end-room` message; the host sees **Keep studio open** and "You are ending this session." Guests' overlays clear. Server test covers cancel, guest refusal, idempotence, and ending again. |
| **Guests waiting** | A guest arriving in the green room produced no alert; the host had to notice a small "1 waiting" label, which could not be clicked. | Hosts and co-hosts get a "Nica is waiting in the green room" toast; the waiting badge is a button that opens People on the Admit control. Browser-checked. |
| **Invite dialog** | The close icon was invisible: the global 10 × 20 px button padding left a 34 px button no room, so the SVG was 0 px wide. | Padding removed; the icon is 18 px. An app-wide scan found no other collapsed icons. |
| **Workspace attribution** | "Powered by ArnoldFamily.com" linked to the wrong domain. | Links to ArnoldFamini.com. |
| **Release safety** (masterplan F9) | Every push to `main` deployed to production without running any of the 1,300+ tests, and pull requests had no checks. | `deploy.yml` now typechecks and runs every suite before any deploy step. New `ci.yml` runs the same checks and a full build on every pull request. Root `npm test` runs all suites. |

## Working as expected

Studio creation and host entry; green room and admit; video in both directions (1080p local, 480×270 simulcast layer for thumbnails); public chat guest → host; guest leaving updates the host; keyboard shortcut **M**; recording with progressive guest upload ("Uploading · 0%" → "Upload complete"); MP4 export and download; RTMP delivery (1920×1080 H.264 High + AAC 48 kHz received by the listener); watch-page live status and viewer count; no horizontal overflow for a phone guest at 390 px.

## Observed, not confirmed as a product defect

- **Live output timestamps under CPU starvation.** With four cores at load ≈10 (two browsers with 1080p fake cameras plus three 1080p x264 encodes), the browser's WebM arrived in bursts. The media server put the reconnecting slate up five times in 80 s, and the received FLV had forward timestamp jumps of 2–58 s with audio ending about 60 s after video. A controlled reproduction with the real encoder, slate, and splicer (8-second stall with delayed delivery, and 8 seconds of missing video frames) kept audio and video within 20 ms. So the splice is sound in those cases. The drift most likely came from the starved browser encoder. What we know for sure: a host on a weak machine can make the slate appear briefly during a real show. Adding program A/V-offset and timestamp-jump metrics to the relay would let production confirm or rule this out (see roadmap).
- **Watch page in Playwright's Chromium** shows "This browser can't play this broadcast's video format" because that build has no H.264. This is a known test-browser limitation, already handled with a clear message.

## Improvement roadmap toward StreamYard / Riverside parity

Ranked by user impact per effort. Items marked *verified* were seen in this run; the rest continue `docs/MASTERPLAN.md`.

### Quick wins (days)

| # | Improvement | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Go Live panel: destinations first.** Put destination toggles and **Go Live** at the top; fold orientation, quality, the 11-item checklist, and stream screens into "Output settings". *Verified*: **Add Destination** sits below the whole checklist. | StreamYard's go-live flow starts from destinations. Fewer steps before the show. |
| 2 | **Inline "Add to stage" strip** under the stage for backstage and waiting guests, as in StreamYard, instead of requiring the People panel. | Hosts manage guests without leaving the stage view. |
| 3 | **Arrival chime and a browser notification** when the studio tab is hidden. | A toast is missed if the host is on another tab. |
| 4 | **Consistent counts.** *Verified*: the header said "1 in studio" while People said "2 in studio" (header counts on-stage video only). Rename it "1 on stage". | Avoids confusion about who is present. |
| 5 | **Explain disabled buttons.** *Verified*: **Create Studio** and **Join Studio** stay disabled without saying the name is required. | First-run friction. |
| 6 | **Remember the guest's name and devices** on the join page for returning guests. | Faster rejoin. |
| 7 | **Invite dialog order:** guest link first, watch page second. | Inviting a guest is the common action. |
| 8 | **"Mirror my video" setting.** The self-view is mirrored (correct default; the program is not). Some hosts want the true view, e.g. to hold up a Bible or book. | StreamYard offers this setting. |
| 9 | Stop re-requesting catalogs of ended rooms. *Verified*: 4 × 404 on `/recordings/rooms/:id/catalog` after a room ends (memory mode). | Noise in logs and rate limits. |

### Reliability (weeks) — highest value

| # | Improvement | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | **Relay telemetry:** program A/V offset, timestamp jumps, slate activations, and encoder duplicate-frame counts per broadcast, shown in Session Health and exported to Prometheus. | Confirms or rules out the drift observed above, and catches it in real shows. |
| 11 | **Guest uploads that survive the room closing:** request upload tokens over HTTP (not the studio WebSocket), so a guest who leaves or whose room ends still completes the upload. | Removes the reason End has to wait, and closes Riverside's "every track arrives" gap. |
| 12 | Production TURN, PostgreSQL, and object storage, verified on paid hosting (masterplan 0.5–0.7). | Free instances sleep; memory stores reset on restart. |
| 13 | Playwright end-to-end suite in CI: the flows in this report with fake devices. | These checks were manual; they should gate releases. |
| 14 | Separate guest camera and screen tracks (masterplan F10). | StreamYard and Riverside both show a guest on camera while sharing. |
